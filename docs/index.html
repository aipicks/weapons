The Shots on Goal tab uses player shot lines from five sportsbooks: it projects each player's shots (recent form, season rate, ice time, power play time, opponent shots allowed, game pace), converts that to P(over) and P(under) at the line, blends with the de-vigged market, and shows the side with the higher EV. The projection level is calibrated to the market each day, so only the differences between players count. Opponent shots against: rank 1 = fewest allowed (red, tough for shooters). The All Players tab lists only the 30 players with the highest EV across every game on the slate. EV is the expected profit per $1 bet if the model probability is right: model % × payout − 1, at the FanDuel anytime-goal price shown. The model leans on the market itself and is not calibrated, so treat large EV as a lead to check, not a guarantee. On the Tim Hortons tab players are ranked by model probability within each pick pool (Tim Hortons tab) or across every player with anytime-goal odds (All Players tab). Model P(goal) blends the sportsbook anytime-goal consensus (vig removed) with goal-game rate, power-play unit, team implied total, opponent defense and goalie. Weights are starting priors, not fitted. Matchup colors: red = tough for the shooter, green = favorable. Market and goal-game chips compare a player with the rest of his own pool (top third strong/high, bottom third weak/low), so a Pick #3 depth player can be "strong" at 18%. Preseason projected defence ranking is each team's projected defensive-unit rank (1 = best) and is not used in the model. Opponent defense: ranks 1–10 red, 11–20 gray, 21–32 green. Goalie ranks use thirds of qualified goalies (20+ GP last season). * = changed teams since last season. "Projected" goalie means RotoWire lists him but the team has not confirmed.
