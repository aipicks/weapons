Ranks and percentages are within each pick pool. Model P(goal) blends the sportsbook anytime-goal consensus (vig removed) with goal-game rate, power-play unit, team implied total, opponent defense and goalie. Weights are starting priors, not fitted. Matchup colors: red = tough for the shooter, green = favorable. Market and goal-game chips compare a player with the rest of his own pool (top third strong/high, bottom third weak/low), so a Pick #3 depth player can be "strong" at 18%. Preseason projected defence ranking is each team's projected defensive-unit rank (1 = best) and is not used in the model. Opponent defense: ranks 1–10 red, 11–20 gray, 21–32 green. Goalie ranks use thirds of qualified goalies (20+ GP last season). * = changed teams since last season. "Projected" goalie means RotoWire lists him but the team has not confirmed.
